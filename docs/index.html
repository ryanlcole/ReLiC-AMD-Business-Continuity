ReLiC preserves why a state exists, what authority governed it, what changed later, and which records are affected. It exposes work for an AI or authorized human to perform. ReLiC does not perform the operational change itself.

My duties

1 · Preserve authority
Keep the original order as evidence. An amendment can retire authority without erasing the order's existence.
2 · Keep time separate
Track when an event applies, when it became known, when it was corrected, and what is authoritative now.
3 · Reveal affected records
Resolve records intersecting an order, amendment, backdate, retention rule, or correction and explain why each was selected.
4 · Preserve uncertainty
If retroactivity or grandfathering is not established, stop at HUMAN AUDIT REQUIRED instead of inventing an answer.
5 · Never execute
I report consequences and provenance. AI, business systems, and authorized humans own mutations and approvals.
