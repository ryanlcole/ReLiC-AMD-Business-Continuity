Authority records

Canon, rules, laws and memorandums are authority-bearing record types. Title alone does not determine precedence. The canon lists issuer/source, jurisdiction/scope, effective time, explicit precedence, amendment/correction/supersession relationships, applicability and evidence/provenance as evaluation dimensions.

Human-law boundary

Internal AI/project authority is subordinate to applicable human law. The repository does not claim the software can independently determine all applicable law.

Change rule

prior state → evidence/trigger → authority → reason → change → verification → resulting state

Execution boundary

AMD/vLLM execution, AMD hardware-health monitoring/processor tuning and Evolus integration are not claimed until their respective interfaces are connected and verified.

Temporal continuity

app/relic/temporal.py separates valid time from recorded time, resolves applicable rates, tracks supersession and reconciles historical work without rewriting original work/payment records.
