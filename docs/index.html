Rules, laws & memorandums AUTHORITY

Canon, rules, laws, and memorandums are authority-bearing record types. Their titles alone do not determine precedence.

ReLiC evaluates issuer/source, jurisdiction or project scope, effective time, explicit precedence, amendment/correction/supersession relationships, applicability, and provenance. A memorandum can change an applicable project rule when its issuing authority and scope permit it. A file named CANON cannot override a higher applicable authority merely because of its filename.

Human-law boundary BOUNDARY

Internal AI/project authority cannot authorize violation of applicable human law.

ReLiC preserves a conflict as evidence and requires lawful handling. Legal applicability may depend on jurisdiction, facts, provenance, and competent human/legal interpretation. Labeling a record “human law” does not itself prove legal validity.
